| 1 | `case_05` judged COMPLETE although the failover replica still holds `account_005` | A *later, unrelated* inventory snapshot enumerates the account category and does not list the account; I treated "not listed anywhere newer" as proof of absence. The data intentionally makes snapshots about different people look authoritative | Introduced the **anchor file**: the latest file captured <= `audit_at` that mentions the person. | 2 | `case_17` got an extra `account_6523`; later `case_07` got an extra `object_19`, `case_17` an extra `tombstone_17` | The same person appears in two scenarios (a 2026-03 one and a 2026-09 one). | 3 | `case_27` lost its account | The only identified row for the account is a snapshot dated *after* `audit_at` (the account is "resurrected" two days later - a deliberate trap) | Separate **ownership** from **state**: a resource id never changes hands, so any evidence may say whom it belongs to; only evidence at or before `audit_at` may say what state it is in or be cited. `validate_citations` enforces the citation half on the whole output | 0 post-audit citations in the 518 rows |
